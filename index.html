🗺️ Geotagging Tool - Simple Guide

How to add location data to your photos in 5 easy steps

1 Upload Your Photos
Upload files from your camera or phone. If a photo already has EXIF GPS, those coordinates are used immediately. Otherwise the tool falls back to the noexif path: date/time from the filename, then GPX matching or manual pin.
Noexif prerequisite: When EXIF GPS is missing, filenames must include ISO-8601 date/time or a common camera timestamp, e.g. 2025-08-04T13:27Z, 20250804_132744, PXL_20230320_162237500.
📸
2 Upload Your GPS Track
Upload a GPX file from your GPS device, phone app, or fitness tracker. This contains your route with timestamps and coordinates.
📡
3 Automatic Matching
Photos without EXIF GPS are matched to GPX points by filename time. If a photo was taken at 2:30 PM, it finds the GPS location from 2:30 PM in your track. Files that already had EXIF GPS are left as-is.
You can preview the matched media in the 'media viewer' tab.
⚡
4 Manual Adjustments (if needed)
For photos that don't match automatically, click on the map to manually set their location. If a GPX with elevation is loaded and a nearby point exists, the tool adds elevation automatically.
🎯
5 Download Geotagged Photos
Download all your photos (images only) with coordinates (and elevation if available) embedded in the filename — so location survives even when EXIF is stripped. The ZIP also includes a map overview image.
📥
🎉 That's it! Your photos now have location data that will never get lost, even when shared on social media or stored in the cloud. The coordinates are part of the filename, so they're always preserved.
